ABOUT THE IDEA

A community cleanup loop

  1. A student photographs a plant.
  2. The prototype checks whether it looks like a plant and a possible weed.
  3. The student creates a digital location flag.
  4. An adult volunteer confirms and removes it safely.

This classroom prototype is not a botanical or safety tool. Never touch or eat an unknown plant.
